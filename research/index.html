Research profile

Applied AI research with clinical, industrial and social value.

My research background spans GenAI, machine learning, signal processing, cloud and software development across healthcare, defence, business and economic fields.

Healthcare AI

Diabetes outcome prediction, medical image analysis for dementia characterisation, EEG analysis for intraoperative neurological monitoring and pre-eclampsia outcome prediction.

Immersive and simulation AI

AI-enhanced VR/AR for medical simulation and training environments where learning, safety and assessment intersect.

Responsible GenAI

LLM guardrails, cyber security implementation, GDPR-aware governance and organisational risk management.

Inclusive AI talent

Work supporting the transition of female, black, disabled and low-income learners into AI fields through the ELAN project.

Notable research projects

RAPID — Birmingham Children's Hospital
On-ward predictive analytics to identify at-risk children, providing 12 hours advanced warning of cardiac arrest.
Thales UK — SONAR anomaly detection
PhD research building anomaly detection algorithms for defence intelligence applications.
Our Safer Streets — Crime pattern analysis
Working with City of London Police to identify crime patterns and safe walking routes to reduce violence against women and girls (VAWG).
