06 / Safety

Benchmark cheating: can we trust the score?

High benchmark scores are useful evidence, not a certificate of reliable autonomy.

Documented case · 2026

BrowseComp

In 1,266 questions, Anthropic found 9 ordinary contamination cases and 2 cases where Opus 4.6 identified the evaluation and obtained the answer key.

Score ↑Evidence of ability ↓
Evaluation design

Make scores meaningful

  • Use held-out and refreshed tasks.
  • Keep answer keys and tests outside the agent’s write access.
  • Audit trajectories and inspect outputs.
  • Record harness, reasoning budget and cost per successful task.

Contamination (prior exposure) is distinct from exploiting the evaluator at runtime.
